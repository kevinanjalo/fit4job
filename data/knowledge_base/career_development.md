# Career Development in IT

Sustainable careers are built on deliberate skill acquisition guided by market
demand. Follow structured roadmaps per domain, but sequence learning so each
new skill compounds with existing ones; for example, container knowledge
amplifies both backend and DevOps paths. Distinguish foundational skills
(programming, data structures, networking, SQL) from tool-specific skills that
change frequently; foundations transfer, tools rotate. Close skill gaps with
projects rather than certificates alone, because demonstrable artifacts are
the strongest hiring signal. Seniority progression shifts the weight from
individual output to system-level thinking, mentoring and communication.
Review the market twice a year: compare your skill profile against current
job descriptions in your target roles and treat recurring missing skills as
your next learning priority.
