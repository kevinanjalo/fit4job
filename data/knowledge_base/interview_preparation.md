# Technical Interview Preparation

Effective preparation targets three tracks. Coding: practise data structures
and algorithms (arrays, hash maps, trees, graphs, dynamic programming) by
solving problems aloud, stating complexity, and testing edge cases before
declaring completion. System design: rehearse a repeatable framework —
clarify requirements, estimate scale, sketch the high-level architecture,
deep-dive one component, then discuss bottlenecks and trade-offs. Behavioural:
prepare stories in the situation-task-action-result structure covering
conflict, failure, leadership and ambiguity; keep each under two minutes.
When answering role-specific questions, connect your experience directly to
the responsibilities in the job description. Always prepare thoughtful
questions for the interviewer about team practices, on-call load and growth
paths, since interviews are bidirectional evaluations.
